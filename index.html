Questions

Before you integrate

What happens if we go over our monthly export limit?

Requests keep succeeding — we bill overage per 1,000 exports at the rate for your plan rather than rejecting calls. You'll see overage usage on your dashboard in real time and can set an alert threshold.

Does ExportAI store our data?

No. Data is streamed through for the duration of a single export job and discarded once the file is generated. Generated files live behind a signed URL for 15 minutes, then are deleted.

Can we brand the exported files for our own customers?

Yes, on the Sheet and Workbook plans. You can set a logo, header row styling, and filename pattern per template, so files look native to your product, not to ours.

How large a file can a single export produce?

Row supports up to 50,000 rows per file; Sheet up to 1,000,000 rows via streamed generation; Workbook has no fixed cap and is provisioned to your traffic pattern.

Do you offer an annual option?

All plans above are billed monthly with no lock-in. If you want annual billing, our sales team can quote a discounted annual rate on the Workbook plan.
